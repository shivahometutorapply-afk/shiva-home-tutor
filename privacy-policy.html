Information We Collect

When you submit the parent enquiry form or tutor registration form on this website, we collect the details you provide — such as name, mobile number, email, class, subject, area and, for tutors, qualification and experience.

How We Use It

This information is used solely to match parents with suitable tutors and to respond to enquiries. It is saved to a private Google Sheet and/or emailed to our admin address, and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

WhatsApp & Email Communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp or email, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's and your email provider's own privacy practices in addition to ours.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry data as long as reasonably needed to provide our matching service. If you'd like your information removed, contact us at shivahometutor.apply@gmail.com.
