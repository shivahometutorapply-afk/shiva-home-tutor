Home Tutor Jobs & Online Tutor Jobs

Work from home tutor jobs across Gurgaon, Noida, Delhi, Ghaziabad and Faridabad — teach in person or online, on your own schedule.

Searching for home tutor jobs or online tutor jobs you can do from home? Shiva Home Tutor connects qualified tutors with families across Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Ghaziabad and Faridabad. Whether you want a home tutor job teaching in person, an online tutor job over video call, or a mix of both, registration is straightforward — share your details, and if there's a match with current family requirements, we'll reach out.

Types of Tutor Jobs Available

  • Home tutor jobs — visit a family's home for in-person sessions in your preferred area
  • Online tutor jobs — teach over video call, ideal if you want an online tutor job you can do entirely from home
  • Work from home tutor jobs — a genuine work-from-home option for tutors who prefer not to travel
  • Part-time or full-time, depending on how many students and hours you take on

What We Look For

  • Relevant qualification for the subjects and classes you want to teach
  • Prior teaching or tutoring experience (preferred, not always required)
  • Willingness to complete a background verification check
  • Reliable availability for the area and mode (home/online) you register for

How to Apply for a Tutor Job

Fill in the tutor registration form on our homepage with your qualification, experience, subjects, classes, preferred area and mode (home, online, or both). After submitting, email your resume and a recent photo to shivahometutor.apply@gmail.com, or send them on WhatsApp.

What Happens Next

Our team reviews your details and reaches out when a suitable home tutor job or online tutor job matches your profile. There's no cost to register.
